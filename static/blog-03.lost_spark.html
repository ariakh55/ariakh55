Why nothing feels new or real?

There's something in my chest for these past few months since I have moved to another country to make it further possible to achieve my childhood dreams, which I cannot event remember in between the day to day life trying to provide only for myself in a dorm room. This is not going to be technical or a simple article about how something can be used, it's just me (Sulfur) trying to get something out of chest.

I have heard too many people on social media posting about how cooked our generation is. They are not wrong but looking at their opportunities it's really messed up, isn't it? Now looking here, at me, coming from a country where internet access was limited and we had to buy access by either bribing or getting into good side of the person in charge. Education was free but full of unnecessary religious things, which could suck the whole life out of a 12 year old boy.

I was raised into mid-level family but my parents did all they could to raise me and to be a responsible person in the future, among all hurdles and challenges they put all their efforts making sure I was well fed, had good clothings, had fun and others things. Well I remember I had a old pc and a PlayStation 1 and played Ace Combat 1 and Tekken 3 all day and night on a tiny CRT television, and after a quite a good while I acquired the TV as my parents bought the brand new Sony Bravia TV and gave that CRT to me. I never forget how much fun I had with that TV Playing two or three games and mostly watching Garfield and Shrek and other DVDs. Like said earlier my parents did all they could to keep me updated with the world around me, and looked great to me and I'm always grateful for it. But there's a limit to what a person can do.

As I got older and became an adult myself I always look back at those time ca. 2004 - 2006, I am filled a warmth that I cannot feel again. Everything back then was real, I had friends, neighbors, classmates although I was (and still am) shy (not introverted) but I had a good time. I went to my friends place and they came to mine and we played all the times, we cycled, and other stuff. I had some great times growing up until we had to move to another part of town for my middle school when I was 12. I lost contact with all of my childhood friends and I was ready to go through puberty.

Being a new in the new town everything felt weird at first but slowly I got used to it and made my way across it and found a new friend which I don't quite exactly know how I managed to keep a good contact with him up until now. Those times went by fast and was ready to go to high school. And with a sudden twist I went and chose to go to the vocational school instead of regular high school which forced the students to study physics and math for three years straights.

The first day of my high school was diabolical. This time of my life I had to wake up on 5 am morning to get ready to get to the subway station which was unfinished and I had to walk for 15 minutes to get to that station, and make sure not to miss the morning train. If I missed it then I would have to wait an estimated time of 30 minutes for the next train. All those things aside, I went to that station with my dad and then we waited for the train to come then my dad sent me off and waved at me. I was like WHAT?! never having to go anywhere remotely longer than 20 minutes from home, that was a huge shock to me. Well anyways I also survived that. But among all these I went through with my family's support I wish I could have stayed in those times especially my high school days.

Now when I'm in working class and a fully fledged adult and finally managed to get overseas (not a first world country btw); I feel overwhelmed, constantly tired and nothing feels the same and most importantly real to me. It's been months that I think to myself, why nothing feels new or even real? There are many ways to have that question addressed. And by addressing I mean we can't really answer it and we just have to address it through our daily lives in the little window of time we have to ourselves to think about things or not to think at all. Some people say it was from the times of Covid, some say it was from the rapid technological advancements. Many believe that the social media and bunch of other craps. Same old people trying to blame something. However there's more than that to this time of depression and I think between the ages of 18 to 30, I guess.

If I were to put a blame on something I would have so many things and reasons to make them my scapegoat to escape the reality that I am faced with in order to survive the daily routines of my life. Maybe that's the problem everyday looking the same although trying different types of coffee, eat different breakfast , lunch, and dinner, watching different shows and movies, going out. It feels cold cause it seems like a never ending cycle of working and doing absolutely zero to nothing in the free-time other than doom scrolling or playing games to fill the blank/void in the life. Compared to past that void was filled with joy and warmth with things that yet to be explored but nowadays there are information everywhere. I used to open my flip phone just to send a joke to my friends when I went out to malls with my parents. Now you look at your phone notifications everywhere, Reddit, Twitter, LinkedIn (which I hate the most). Back we had to wait to get home to connect to a ADSL which had noise and a limited speed to open GOOGLE and search for Afashin's new song and download the mp3.

The other thing that is eating me is that I consistently compare myself to my parents, they founded their family at my age and dealt with every challenge head on and managed to raise me and keep the family steady. Looking at myself, I'm living alone in a hostel room trying to get my shit together to land a job somewhere with a steady economy and if God allows (which I'm not so sure of) to found my own family (which I won't, cause of family trauma).

In conclusion having I'm getting older and older and there's no way to go back even a fraction of second and this darkness and coldness haunts me time to time and makes me think what a miserable person I have become. If I had the solution I would have written it here to share my experience, but going through it and at the same time racing with the pace of life, makes it survival not an adventure like the great time I had at school. But there's one thing that comes to my mind every time I have these thoughts and that makes it a little bit easier for me to deal with is the saying "If you can't make it today, there's always tomorrow". So with this darkness I keep on keeping on to overcome this.

Find your warmth and never lose it. And don't you dare go hollow.
